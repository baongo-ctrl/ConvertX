Why Vietnam is different

Vietnamese B2B buyers research online, then decide through trust

Business buyers in Vietnam validate solutions online long before they speak to sales, then make the final decision through relationships and trust. Foreign SaaS companies often get the first half right and lose deals in the second: the demo goes well, then the deal goes quiet.

  • An online presence credible enough to earn the first meeting, in Vietnamese and English
  • Follow-up on the channels Vietnamese buyers actually use, including Zalo
  • An offline experience (meetings, events, local references) that matches what buyers saw online
Translating your website is not market entry. Vietnam rewards SaaS companies that localise trust: local proof, local channels and a sales process built for multi-stakeholder decisions.
