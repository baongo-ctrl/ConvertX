The problem

Plenty of sign-ups, too few qualified buyers

This is the most common bottleneck we see in B2B SaaS: the trial and demo numbers look healthy, but most leads are students, freelancers or very small accounts. Sales wastes time, real decision-makers wait, and multi-stakeholder deals stall after the demo.

No idea which lead to call first

Every sign-up lands in one list, so sales works it top to bottom.

Demos that go quiet

Without timed follow-up for every stakeholder, good demos don’t turn into proposals.

Marketing and sales in silos

Marketing reports leads; sales reports deals; nobody owns the gap in between.

Channel spend you can’t trace

Ads, content and events run separately, with no view of which one drives revenue.
