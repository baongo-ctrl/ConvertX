FAQ

Why not just hire a freelancer?

A freelancer is often cheaper for a single task. What’s the difference?

If you need one fix, such as a single workflow or one landing page, a freelancer may be the right call. ConvertX builds end-to-end conversion systems: strategy, CRM and automation, and execution working together. One task fixed in isolation usually leaves the rest of the funnel leaking. Our free audit shows you where it leaks, so you can decide what you actually need.

Why “from” prices instead of a fixed price list?

Scope depends on your CRM, your data, and your channels. Each card shows the starting price and exactly what’s included. Every engagement starts with a written scope and price before any work begins.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most teams start with the free Funnel Audit, then a fixed-scope CRM + Automation project. If that works, you can move to Growth Engine or Growth Loop.

Can’t we just do this ourselves with AI tools?

AI tools can write content and draft workflows. They don’t decide which leads matter, connect your CRM to your follow-up, or hold anyone accountable for pipeline. That’s the part we own.

How do international clients pay?

We can contract and invoice through Upwork, which handles payment protection and billing.

Which CRMs do you work with?

HubSpot, Zoho, and ClickUp.

Who have you worked with?

B2B teams across SaaS, eCommerce, professional services, and EdTech, including ClassIn, cirCO, Wego Coffee, OKR Business, and Firegroup.
