Example workflow

An example: automated demo follow-up for a SaaS company

STEP 01

Demo booked

A prospect books a demo on your website. The CRM creates or updates the lead and scores it.

STEP 02

Reminder

The day before, the prospect receives a reminder message via Zalo (where they’ve agreed to it) and email.

STEP 03

After the demo

A thank-you message with the recording and next steps goes out automatically. The account owner gets a task.

STEP 04

Proposal

The proposal and pricing are delivered via Zalo OA. Opens and replies are logged.

STEP 05

Nudge or escalate

No reply in a set number of days? The system nudges the buyer or alerts sales to call.

Every step is visible in your CRM, so marketing and sales see the same pipeline. Workflows are customised to your sales cycle and to Zalo’s current messaging rules.

For foreign companies

Can a foreign company use a Zalo Official Account?

Yes, with some preparation. According to Zalo’s own documentation, creating and managing an OA requires a verified Vietnamese (+84) Zalo account, and a verified business OA requires Vietnamese business registration documents. Zalo’s rules change, so check the current requirements at oa.zalo.me before you start.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Zalo be integrated with a CRM?

Yes. A Zalo Official Account can be connected to CRMs such as HubSpot, Zoho or ClickUp through Zalo’s APIs or integration tools, so leads, messages and statuses are logged in one pipeline.

Can a foreign company create a Zalo Official Account?

According to Zalo’s documentation, creating and managing an OA requires a verified Vietnamese (+84) Zalo account, and a verified business OA requires Vietnamese business registration. Foreign companies usually use a local entity or agree OA ownership with a local partner. Check the current requirements at oa.zalo.me.

What can be automated on Zalo for B2B sales?

Common automations include instant replies to enquiries, demo reminders, post-demo follow-up, proposal and pricing delivery, and alerts to sales when a lead goes quiet, all logged in your CRM.

Is Zalo automation the same as spamming customers?

No. We only message people who have agreed to hear from you, keep messages relevant to where they are in the buying process, and follow Zalo’s template and content rules.

Which CRMs do you support?

We regularly work with HubSpot, Zoho and ClickUp, and can assess other CRMs during the audit.

How much does Zalo automation and CRM integration cost?

Our CRM + Automation Optimization project is 30–50 million VND, depending on scope. Zalo’s own OA and messaging fees are separate and paid to Zalo.
