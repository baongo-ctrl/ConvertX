FAQ

Frequently asked questions

We don't have budget for a long-term program yet.

Start with the free Funnel Audit. You know where your funnel leaks before you spend anything, then decide whether you need a project or an ongoing program.

Why not just hire a freelancer?

If you need one fix, such as a single workflow or one landing page, a freelancer may be the right call. ConvertX builds end-to-end conversion systems: strategy, CRM, automation and execution working together. One task fixed in isolation usually leaves the rest of the funnel leaking.

Can't we just do this ourselves with AI tools?

AI tools can write content and draft workflows. They don't decide which leads matter, connect your CRM to your follow-up, or hold anyone accountable for pipeline. That's the part we own.

Which CRMs do you work with?

HubSpot, Zoho and ClickUp.
